## What the Numbers Mean

The scorer estimates the fraction of households in its **modeled reference distribution** that would use at least a measured monthly volume. “1 in N” is its reciprocal, not an observed subscriber count or ISP warning. Directional tails have less validation. The 1Q27 reference is a projection, and rare estimates depend heavily on the Pareto tail.
